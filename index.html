A LITTLE BIT ABOUT US

SICILIAN
ROOTS.
MANCHESTER
HEART.

Marleo began as a street-food spot in Pollard Yard. Maria and Leo have since brought their Sicilian favourites to a sunny café and deli on Oldham Street in the Northern Quarter.

It's all about the simple things: a sandwich made with care, a coffee at the counter, and a little taste of home in the city.
